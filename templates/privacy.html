Privacy & Data

What this app collects, and what it does not.

Uploaded audio

Your uploaded audio file is used only to run the analysis you requested. It is deleted immediately after analysis completes, whether the analysis succeeds or fails. No audio is stored on our servers, and recordings are never retained, shared, or reused.

Personal data

No personal data is collected, sold, or shared. The app does not require an account, and it does not ask for or store your name, email, or any other identifying information.

IP addresses

Your IP address is temporarily logged for rate limiting only — it is used to prevent abuse by capping how many uploads a single source can make in a given window. It is not used to profile or identify you, and is not sold or shared with anyone.

AI-generated analysis

Raaga analysis and wellness information are generated by AI for educational purposes only, and are not medical or therapeutic advice.
